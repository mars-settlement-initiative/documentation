Mesa and multi-agent systems: Mars Colony
==================================================

Explore a working Mesa simulation, then investigate how individual robot
decisions affect the colony through two exercises.


.. image:: assets/mission-01-baseline.gif
   :scale: 90 %
   :alt: alternate text
   :align: right




.. list-table:: Your learning route
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Folder
     - Task
     - Evidence of learning
   * - ``mars-colony-foundation``
     - Introduction: explore the code. No coding required.
     - Explain a simulation tick and record baseline observations.
   * - ``mars-colony-robot-decisions``
     - Exercise 1: change Python decisions in ``agents.py``.
     - Explain and evaluate your rule changes.
   * - ``mars-colony-llm``
     - Exercise 2: edit the strategy in ``prompt.txt``.
     - Evaluate prompt-driven collector choices using results and logs.

Keep mars-colony-foundation unchanged as your
reference. mars-colony-robot-decisions starts with equivalent baseline behaviour. mars-colony-llm adds
a collector decision interface to the same world; your mars-colony-robot-decisions edits are
not automatically copied into it.
